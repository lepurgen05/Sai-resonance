Why “Vahini”?

The Vahinis are writings by Sri Sathya Sai Baba covering spiritual wisdom, dharma, meditation, divine love, Vedanta, education, Rama and Krishna, and questions about spiritual life.

ABOUT SAI RESONANCE

A place to read, reflect and remember.

Sai Resonance is a digital literature and reflection space created to bring together Chinna Katha, Sai literature, Vahini studies, teachings and daily reflections in one simple place.

The summaries and descriptions in the Vahini Library are intended as study aids. For the complete text, readers should use the linked official publications and sources.

Sai Resonance is an educational and literature project and is not an official website of the Sri Sathya Sai organizations.
