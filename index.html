The Two Seeds

ABOUT SAI RESONANCE

A small digital space for a lasting thought.

Sai Resonance is designed as a calm, accessible collection of stories, literature, teachings and daily reflections. The goal is not to overwhelm you with information, but to give you one meaningful idea to carry forward.

The daily cards on this starter site are devotional reflections created for the project. They should not be treated as verbatim quotations from Sri Sathya Sai Baba unless a source is explicitly provided.
